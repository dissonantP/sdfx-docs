# Pre-SDF tab

This tab is totally optional and just contains some fun, experimental effects which can produce interesting results when fed into the SDF / Post-SDF parts of the algorithm.

As with the Prep tab docs, I will be showing some stuff here from those other tabs, even though I haven't covered them in detail yet - just roll with me.

<img src="/images/pre-sdf-ui.png" alt="img">

We'll continue to use our Silverfish insect example here. We've applied some preprocessing in the prep tab to simplify the shape, and turned SDF expansion distance all the way down so basically all we see is the modified input shape:
